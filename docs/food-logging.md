# Food logging

The Food tab (`/food`) logs Indian home food the way people eat it: by the katori, roti, glass
and ladle, with light, normal or rich oil, as a whole thali, and the usual meal in one tap.
Logging and daily totals are free; Pro (`food_insights`) adds real maintenance, food trends on
Progress, protein help with Apply, and typing a meal in one line.

## Where the numbers come from

- **Ingredients**: USDA SR28 values per 100 g (public domain), in
  `src/data/library/ingredients.ts`, generated by `scripts/usda-ingredients.mjs` from the SR28
  abbreviated file (`ABBREV.txt`, also in the npm package `fda-nutrient-database@1.0.2`). Each
  carries its NDB number. Proxies are named in a `note` (paneer uses queso blanco, poha uses
  white rice).
- **Dishes**: standard home recipes in grams, in `src/data/library/foods.ts`. Per 100 g cooked
  = sum of ingredient nutrients / cooked weight of the pot. Breads and batters get their cooked
  weight from moisture (34% for breads, as USDA chapati). The cooking fat is kept apart so the
  oil level changes only the fat (0.5, 1, 2 times).
- **Portions**: volume x fill x density (`unitGrams` in `domain/nutrition/calc.ts`). Katori
  150, 200, 250 ml served 80% full; a person can set their own katori size.
- Nothing is copied from INDB or IFCT. They are used to cross-check and are credited.
  Permission emails were drafted for both; the feature does not depend on them.

Every food is tested (`domain/nutrition/library.test.ts`): energy matches protein, carbs and
fat within 12%, realistic portions, oil levels in order, diet labels from ingredients, and
USDA cooked references for rice and chicken. Change a recipe: bump `FOOD_LIBRARY_VERSION`;
logs keep the numbers they were saved with.

## Data

- Dexie v4: `foodLogs` and `customFoods`. Profile gains `dietType` and `katoriMl`.
- Supabase migration `20261021000011_food_logging.sql` (RLS on both tables). **Apply it in
  the SQL editor before pushing the code.**
- Writes go through `data/repositories/food.ts` (`logFoods`, `copyFoodLogs`, `updateFoodLog`,
  `moveFoodLog`, `deleteFoodLogs`, `restoreFoodLogs`, `saveCustomFood`).

## How it connects

- Home: Food today card with the usual meal for the time of day.
- Body: targets card shows the food log average and real maintenance (Pro) with a target for
  the goal in one tap.
- Progress: Food section (Pro), with an Explain button.
- Workout summary: Refuel card on the day of the workout.
- OverloadGPT: the chat and the targets Explain include food lines (`nutritionLines` in
  `features/coach/brief.ts`).
- Science page: topic `food-values`. Settings: Food CSV export.
