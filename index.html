Questions

Is Overload free?
Yes. Logging, routines, your full history and every record are free with no limit. Pro is a paid upgrade for longer trends and coaching tools.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Overload runs in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen like any other app, even offline.
Can I move my data from Hevy or Strong?
Yes. Export your workouts from Hevy or Strong and import the file in Overload. Importing the same file twice adds nothing new.
Does it use kg or lb?
Either. Choose your unit in settings. Dumbbell weights are entered per dumbbell, and volume counts both hands.
What happens to my data?
It is kept on your phone and in your account so it is never lost. We do not sell your data or show ads. You can export it as CSV or delete your account at any time.
How are the numbers calculated?
From your logged sets only, with published methods. Estimates such as one rep max are labelled as estimates, and nothing is made up to fill a chart.
